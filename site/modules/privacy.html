Privacy

Updated November 18, 2026

Contact messages

The contact form sends your name, email address, and message to the project’s Node.js service. When delivery is configured, the service forwards the message to the four team members via SMTP. Your email address lets the team reply. The service uses the connecting network address to limit repeated submissions.

Dashboard sign-in

The protected dashboard uses a first-party session cookie to keep you signed in and enforce your account role. Your browser also stores preferences such as dashboard appearance and telemetry view.

Questions about your information

For questions about contact messages, hosting logs, the email provider, or retention, contact the project team.
