Debug controls are locked until the bridge has a fresh ESP32 command target.

Supported debug devices: BMS 0x08, PV 0x40, and MPPT 0x6B. ACDRV commands preserve unrelated register bits and require readback; physical PACK voltage must still be confirmed at the hardware.

RGB LED control targets the ESP32-S3-DevKitC-1 v1.1 addressable LED on GPIO 38. Initial board revisions require changing the firmware setting to GPIO 48 before flashing.
